KAIJU EXTRAS

Kaijugotchi (Wi-Fi menu)
  A pet that watches nearby networks.
  Deauths only networks in
  /BrucePCAP/owned_aps.txt.
Captures (Kaijugotchi)
  Select on the Kaijugotchi screen,
  then Captures to open handshakes.
Kaiju Stomp (Apps)
  Run the city. Jump with ; or Select.
  Breathe fire with /.
Kaiju Shooter (Apps)
  Fly and burn the saucers. Prev/Next
  to move, Select to breathe fire.
Kaiju Pong (Apps)
  Bat the fireball past the rival.
  Prev/Next to move, Select for menu.
Kaiju Proximity (Apps)
  Counts nearby phones and Wi-Fi by
  signal strength. Full MAC addresses
  are never shown. M marks your own.
